Life on Mars

15 Min Read 10 Vocab Words

Reading Material

Mars is the fourth planet from the sun. It is often called the "Red Planet" because of its color. Scientists are looking for water on Mars. They want to know if humans can build a colony there in the future.

The atmosphere on Mars is very thin. We cannot breathe the air. If we go to Mars, we will need special suits to survive.

Mars, known colloquially as the "Red Planet," is the fourth planet from the sun and currently the primary focus for human interplanetary exploration. Researchers are actively seeking subterranean ice to determine the viability of establishing a permanent human colony.

The atmosphere is predominantly composed of carbon dioxide and is exceptionally thin, making it inhospitable for human respiration without advanced life-support systems.
